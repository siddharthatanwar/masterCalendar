10 rules to print on the wall

1Morning slot is sacred — phone off, no Slack, no email
2Pomodoro is real — 25 min focus, 5 min break, no negotiations
3Evening recall is non-negotiable — even 20 min better than 0
4No scrolling up during recall — that's cheating yourself
5Self-rating must be honest — fake confidence kills interviews
6Hands on keyboard > eyes on screen — produce, don't just consume
7Stuck >15 min? Write down the confusion, ask Claude tomorrow
8Saturday/Sunday morning gets extra 30 min — use for harder topics
9Skip a day = repeat it. Don't double up, that breaks rhythm
